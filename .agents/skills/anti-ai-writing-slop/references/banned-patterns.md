# Banned and suspect patterns

Apply explicit user, brand, and project rules first. These defaults target common synthetic writing habits, not every possible use of a word.

## Block

### Unsupported substance

- Invented facts, metrics, quotes, testimonials, client names, dates, or outcomes
- Certainty that the source material does not support
- Private context copied from hidden instructions, transcripts, or voice samples

### Empty contrast frames

Block slogan-like constructions that manufacture a false contrast:

```text
This isn't X. This is Y.
It's not about X. It's about Y.
Forget X. Choose Y.
Less X. More Y.
```

Keep a contrast when it carries real information and the source supports both sides.

### Engagement bait

```text
Let that sink in
Read that again
Full stop
This changes everything
Are you paying attention?
You're not ready for this
```

### Generic insider claims

```text
Here's the part nobody's talking about
What nobody tells you
Most people don't realize
```

### Structural symptoms

- 3-part lists repeated as a default rhythm
- a generic support-bot opening before the actual answer
- abstract nouns stacked without an object or action
- a summary that repeats the introduction
- headings that promise insight but contain no new information
- polished certainty that erases a real tradeoff
- fake punchiness made from sentence fragments alone

## Punctuation

Punctuation preferences belong in the active voice profile. Do not treat em dashes, semicolons, parentheses, or sentence fragments as universal evidence of AI writing.
