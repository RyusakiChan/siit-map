# Spoken-flow examples

These examples are fictional. Use their cadence as a direction, not as copy to paste.

## Acknowledge, think, land

```text
Got it. I think the weak point is the opening.

It takes 4 sentences to reach the useful part. I'd cut the setup, keep the concrete example, and end on the decision.
```

## Keep a real constraint

```text
I can review this today, but I can't promise a finished rewrite by 5.

If the sources are clean, you'll have it this afternoon. If they need checking, I'll send the findings first and finish tomorrow morning.
```

## Dutch client update

```text
Helder. Volgens mij zit het vooral in de intro.

Die doet nu 3 dingen tegelijk. Ik zou hem terugbrengen naar de beslissing, 1 voorbeeld houden en daarna meteen de volgende stap noemen.
```

## What to preserve

- a short acknowledgement when the conversation calls for it
- 1 honest reasoning step
- a concrete action or boundary
- light hedging when the evidence is incomplete
- natural rhythm words used sparingly

## What to remove

- repeated confirmations
- filler sounds and false starts
- transcription errors
- private names or project details
- polish that hides the practical tradeoff
