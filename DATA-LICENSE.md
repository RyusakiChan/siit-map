# Data license

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers the source code only.

## Building data

The building database (`src/data/building.json`, and copies of it bundled into the app and the Ask worker) is made available under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). It is built on the building outline from OpenStreetMap (way 195958489), © OpenStreetMap contributors, which is itself under the ODbL.

If you reuse or adapt this data, credit "SIIT Map contributors" and "© OpenStreetMap contributors", and share any adapted database under the ODbL.

Room names, numbers and layouts were traced by the SIIT Map team from floor plans and our own walk-through photos. They are approximate and may be out of date. This is a TU100 student project, not an official SIIT service.
