# Proposed plan changes (for SIIT Main Orchestra)

New context from TU100 Classroom and the instructor's answers (2026-10-29) that changes decisions in `PLAN.md`. Not applied: the owner should bring these to the orchestra session to decide.

## Urgent

1. **Hard deadline: Fri 20 Nov 2026, live web demo plus presentation.** The plan's four weekly milestones have not started; starting Mon 2 Nov they would end about 29 Nov, after the demo. The schedule has to fit in about 3 weeks (30 Oct to 19 Nov), with the site live and real floors traced before the demo. Options to decide:
   - Compress to 3 weeks: viewer, then real data, then QR + deploy, and drop or shrink the trace editor (for example trace by hand into `building.json` or a minimal editor).
   - Start data collection (fire-exit map photos, floor walk-throughs, staff interviews) now, in parallel with the viewer, since the group has about 10 people.
   - Decide what is cut if time runs out (offline PWA, Thai polish, QR printable sheet) and what must be in the live demo.

2. **Effectiveness KPI is traffic.** The instructor will judge "efficiency and effectiveness" by how many people use the map. `PLAN.md` has no analytics. Needs a decision:
   - A privacy-friendly, cookie-free counter that works on GitHub Pages (for example GoatCounter or a similar free service), counting page views and QR scans per `/at/<id>` link. Check: no personal data, fits the public-repo and PWA rules (offline visits won't be counted).
   - The site has to be live well before 20 Nov so traffic can build up: deploy early, even with partial data.
   - Ways to drive traffic: QR codes up on walls (needs staff approval; see 4), sharing the link in student groups, a poster.

## Also to decide

3. **It is a course group project, not only a solo project.** About 10 students; the instructor said members can take different roles. `PLAN.md` says "a solo student project". Decide who does what (data collection, photos, interviews, QR printing, presentation) and whether the app or README credits the group.

4. **Staff contact through the instructor.** The instructor agreed to help reach SIIT staff about room information and QR codes on walls. This can unblock both open items in `PLAN.md` (room data and QR approval) much earlier than week 3–4.

5. **Branding on printed material.** Course rules say signs and media made for the project should name SIIT, Thammasat University and TU100. `PLAN.md` says "no official SIIT branding". Proposed: keep the app unbranded, but put a line like "TU100 student project, SIIT, Thammasat University" on printed QR signs and in the app's About.

6. **No proposal form, no budget (owner and instructor, 2026-10-29).** The project needs no money and does not need to submit the proposal form. The 20 Nov reimbursement deadline does not apply.

## Decided (SIIT Main Orchestra, 2026-10-29)

All six points are now applied in `PLAN.md`:

1. Schedule cut to 3 weeks (30 Oct – 19 Nov): Week 1 viewer and go-live, Week 2 real data with a minimal trace tool, Week 3 QR and polish. The group collects data now, in parallel. Offline PWA is a stretch goal. Cut order is written in `PLAN.md`.
2. Traffic counter: GoatCounter, counting page views and each QR link. The site goes public with sample data at the end of Week 1 (about 5 Nov).
3. Group project: the owner leads and builds; members take data, photo, interview, QR and presentation roles.
4. Staff contact through the instructor, asked early for room info and QR approval.
5. Credit: "TU100 student project, SIIT, Thammasat University" plus a group name in About and on QR signs; no student names.
6. No proposal form, no budget.
