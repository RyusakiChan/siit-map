# TU100 Civic Engagement (course context)

SIIT Map is being built as part of the final project (Project 2) of **TU100 Civic Engagement** (มธ.100 พลเมืองกับการมีส่วนร่วม), section TU100-1, semester 1/2026, Thammasat University / SIIT. 3 credits.

Source: the course's Google Classroom (week 1 overview slides, week 9 slides, class posts), read 2026-10-29. Summarised in our own words; the original files stay in Classroom.

## What the course is about

- Learning to act as a citizen in a democratic system at every level: university, local, national and global.
- Taking part in solving real social problems, starting from your own actions.
- Taught as a participatory class: activity-, problem- and project-based learning, framed as service learning.

### The "six civic qualities" the course keeps returning to

1. Respect the laws (rules)
2. Respect others' rights
3. Respect differences
4. Respect equality
5. Self-dependence (self-reliance)
6. Social responsibility

### Expected learning outcomes

- **Knowledge:** basic principles of democracy; the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
- **Skills:** brainstorming, communication and presentation, critical thinking and conflict resolution, problem-solving and creativity, teamwork and project planning.
- **Attitude:** respect for rules, others' rights, differences and equality; self-dependence and social responsibility.

## Course journey

Why democracy → civic citizenship (six qualities) → global/local problems and project development → project proposal → implementation → project presentation → reflection and learning summary.

## Grading

| Part | Weight | What counts |
|---|---|---|
| Participation, activities, assignments | 45% (20% / 25%) | Attendance and class participation; assignments |
| Two projects | 45% (Project 1 15%, Project 2 30%) | Proposal; efficiency and effectiveness; participation and outside involvement; implementation and presentation |
| Learning summary | 10% | Final exam |

Missing more than 30% of classes (more than 4 times) makes the final exam score 0.

## Projects so far

- **Project 1, "Trash Journey"** (Sept 2026): groups surveyed an assigned campus site's waste handling, from source to truck, after a visit to the TU Recycle Center; found causes and proposed activities. Two submission rounds (11 and 18 Sept).
- **Project 2** (from 9 Oct 2026): a campus problem chosen by each group, **not a waste problem**. This is where SIIT Map fits; the instructor accepted it on 29 Oct. See [tu100-project2-brief.md](tu100-project2-brief.md).

Past semesters' projects worked with outside communities (Klong Ma Don, Ban Khlong Sam, On Nut 14 Rai); semester 2/2025 used the TU / SIIT area, like this semester.
