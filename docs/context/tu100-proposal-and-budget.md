# TU100 proposal form and budget rules

From the "TU100 Project proposal form 1-2026" and "เกณฑ์การเบิกเงินโครงงาน 1-2026" PDFs posted in Classroom on 16 Oct 2026. Summarised; the originals stay in Classroom.

## Proposal form (must be signed by the section instructor)

Header: room, group, instructor, project title, type of project: **TU Project** or **Community Project**. SIIT Map is a TU Project (on campus).

1. Why do we do this project?
   1. Current problem and situation.
   2. How the problem relates to us, or how we are involved in its cause.
   3. Will the project solve it? How?
2. What do you do in the project? (brief description)
3. Expected outcome.
4. When will it take place? (schedule)
5. Budget plan: up to 10 line items, materials only. **Limit: 1,500 baht for a TU Project** (2,500 for a Community Project). Signed by the project head.
6. Member list: head, vice head, secretary, then members, with attendance checks.

The instructor then signs an approval with the approved amount.

## Reimbursement rules (TU Project)

- Up to **1,500 baht per project**, materials only.
- Claim at the ISI department office, 3rd floor, Main Building. **Deadline: Fri 20 Nov 2026, 16:00.**
- Needs: copy of the project head's student card; a full tax invoice or a cash bill that meets the rules; the complete, instructor-signed proposal form; photos if any.
- **Full tax invoice:** shop's tax ID, name, address and phone; issued to SIIT (สถาบันเทคโนโลยีนานาชาติสิรินธร) at the SIIT address and tax ID given in the rules PDF.
- **Cash bill:** seller's name and address (printed or stamped, not a business card), date, itemised list with quantities and prices, total in numbers and words, seller's signature.
- **Not claimable:** prizes, food, snacks, travel, donations, delivery fees.
- Signs, vinyl banners and other media made for the project should say it is a project by SIIT, Thammasat University students in course TU100 (name or logo of SIIT, plus "TU100").

## What this means for SIIT Map

- **Instructor (2026-10-29): SIIT Map does not need to submit the proposal form.** The form above is kept only as a checklist of questions the project should be able to answer.
